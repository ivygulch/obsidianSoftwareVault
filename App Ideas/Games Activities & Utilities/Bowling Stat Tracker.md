# Bowling Stat Tracker

**One-liner:** Pin-by-pin scoring and leave statistics for league bowlers, with Apple Watch entry and no forced subscription.
**Target user:** League and serious recreational bowlers. USBC reported 1,075,194 members and 29,026 certified leagues in 2024–25, flat across four seasons.
**Problem:** Lanetalk, the category leader, put manual entry behind a $7.99-a-month paywall; its 100 most recent reviews average 2.84 against 3.84 lifetime, and 34 of the 47 low ones are about price. PinPal, the paid standard at $8.99, has not changed since March 2023, and its users ask for Watch entry, sync, export, and stat drill-down.
**Differentiator:** Apple Watch pin entry, which no tracker's store listing mentions; drill-down from any stat to its games; iCloud sync and CSV export; a lifetime price beside an annual one.

## The Three Questions

**Who pays, how often, how much?**
A league bowler, once or annually. The market charges $8.99 one-time at the bottom, $20–$40 a year in the middle, and up to $89.99 a year at the top; four newer entrants sell lifetime tiers at $24.99–$59.99. The objection in reviews is to paying monthly for manual entry, not to paying. Estimated range, net: about $200 a month (low), about $1,300 (mid, already a top-quintile assumption), about $6,300 (high). All estimates.

**Year-2 maintenance cost:**
About 40–80 hours a year (estimate): iOS and watchOS updates, bug fixes, support mail. No content if balls and oil patterns stay free-text fields.

**Kill criteria:**
- Lanetalk restores a free tier for manual entry.
- One of the 14 entrants since August 2025 ships Apple Watch entry and passes about 500 ratings.
- PinPal returns with an update covering sync, export, and Watch.
- League bowlers shown a prototype do not value Watch entry enough to switch.

## Concept

Frame entry by pins with league, tournament, and open-play separation; strike and spare percentages by exact leave; ball arsenal with notes; lane, pattern, and session notes; month-by-month averages; no account and no ads. A PinPal import is possible (a rival sells one for $5.99) but the backup format was not examined.

## Prior Art

- **Lanetalk** — auto-imports scores from 1,599 connected centers; $25–$50 a year. Unbeatable where a center is connected; resented for the paywall.
- **PinPal** — $8.99 paid, 5,016 ratings, 90 of its recent 100 at 4–5 stars. Loved, frozen, has returned from a hiatus before.
- **Bosc** — $39.99 a year, 492 US ratings, the largest manual tracker of the last three years; strong in Japan.
- **StrikeMaster, Bowlski, LaneTrax** — 2025–2026 entrants; two ship AI scoreboard scanning, and StrikeMaster sells a PinPal migration add-on.

## Why It Could Work

- A paid incumbent with 5,016 ratings proves bowlers pay for a tracker.
- The leader is pushing users away on price.
- Apps with 57–64 ratings already reach the top five for "bowling score" and "bowling tracker".
- Watch entry is unclaimed.

## Why It Might Not

- Fourteen manual trackers have launched since August 2025; none has passed 64 US ratings.
- PinPal's users are satisfied and owe nothing more.
- Lanetalk's center network is a moat, and one pricing change removes the push.
- The participant base is flat now and far below its historical peak.
- No tracker market shows in the UK, Germany, or France.

## Open Questions

- How do bowlers enter frames today at the lane: phone in hand, or after the game?
- Is PinPal's backup format readable?
- What share of league centers are Lanetalk-connected in the US?
