# Vehicle Fuel & Service Log

**One-liner:** A fuel-economy and maintenance log with reminders that fire, history you can edit, and no account.
**Target user:** Owners who track fuel economy and service history for cars, trucks, motorcycles, and equipment; households sharing vehicles.
**Problem:** Two large incumbents are broken at once. Vehicle Maintenance Tracker (2,927 ratings) has 79 of its 100 most recent reviews at 1–2 stars from a bug that reset all service dates. Fuelly (29,557 ratings) has had no release since June 2024; a quarter of its recent reviews report login errors, reminders that do not fire, and history that cannot be edited.
**Differentiator:** Local store with iCloud sync and household sharing; editable, backdatable history; mileage- and date-based reminders; Fuelly CSV import; kWh entries for EVs; free CSV export.

## The Three Questions

**Who pays, how often, how much?**
A vehicle owner, once. One-time prices of $4.99–$6.99 are praised in reviews; subscriptions are resisted even at $7.99 a year. Estimated range, net: about $95 a month (low), about $570 (mid), about $2,300 (high, which needs a rating rate no post-2017 iOS entrant here has reached). All estimates.

**Year-2 maintenance cost:**
About 40–80 hours a year (estimate): one pass per iOS release, import-format fixes, migration support mail. A data-corruption bug would be fatal in this category.

**Kill criteria:**
- Fuelly ships its rewritten app (staff-confirmed in progress; beta slipped past December 2025).
- A side-by-side with Road Trip MPG shows no gap worth a user's switch.
- Vehicle Maintenance Tracker ships a fix and its review stream recovers.

## Concept

Fill-up entry with partial and missed fills; service records with photo and PDF attachments; per-vehicle units; hour-meter equipment; widgets and a "log fill-up" App Intent. No vehicle database, so no yearly content update. A full CarPlay app fits no entitlement category on a plain reading; a widget is the available surface.

## Prior Art

- **Fuelly** — 29,557 ratings, $7.99–$10.99 a year, stale. Its community MPG database is a network effect that zero of 100 recent reviewers mention.
- **Road Trip MPG** — $6.99 one-time, 627 ratings, 95 of its recent 100 at 4–5 stars. Already has EV support, iCloud sync, export, and Fuelly import. The strongest evidence against this idea.
- **Vehicle Maintenance Tracker** — 2,927 ratings, collapsing since a June 2026 bug.
- **CARFAX Car Care** — 127,591 ratings, free; covers the service half automatically from shop records.
- **Fuelio, Drivvo** — Android-first, 135,748 and 115,316 ratings there.

## Why It Could Work

- Stranded users are asking where to go; Fuelly's own forum has a "what to use next" thread.
- Apps with zero to three ratings appear in the first results for "fuel log" and "mpg tracker".
- No backend, licence, hardware, or content strike.

## Why It Might Not

- A healthy paid rival already covers the feature list.
- Fuelly's rewrite is real.
- Eleven new entrants since 2025, none above 76 ratings.
- The lowest price ceiling of the five finalists.
- The big audiences are on Android, and mixed households want cross-platform sync.
- Fuel logging erodes slowly as EVs and connected-car apps log automatically.

## Open Questions

- What is the state of the Fuelly beta after January 2026?
- Why has Road Trip MPG stayed at 627 ratings: paid-up-front distribution, or limited demand?
- Does Vehicle Maintenance Tracker have an export its users could migrate from?
