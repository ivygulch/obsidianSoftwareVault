# Applying Proverbs — Idea Backlog

Every idea captured so far for [[Applying Proverbs]], grouped by layer. Nothing here is scoped or ranked; the sorting into a long-range plan and a V1 is a separate pass. Items in the "From the card deck" group come from [[Wisdom Deck Reference]].

## Business model

- Core feature free to use.
- Possibly a low-cost subscription for the AI feature: the user describes a situation, the app produces relevant passages.
- Open: whether a one-time purchase belongs anywhere; what the free core includes and what the subscription adds beyond AI.

## Text

- Preferred version: ESV.
- Open to other versions depending on license terms and cost.
- Need: license restrictions for a full book offline, in a freemium app, with an AI tier. See [[Bible Text Licensing & APIs]].

## Structure of the book

- Break the whole book into passages and categorize every one. Unit may be a verse or a multi-verse saying.
- Categories of several different types at once: situations, attitudes, individual characteristics, results, and others to be defined. Draft in [[Categorization Scheme]].
- Keywords per passage.
- Wiki-style links between passages, derived from shared categories, tags, and keywords, plus hand-made links.

## User structure

- Custom tags on any passage.
- User-created groupings for longer-term background or context: an ongoing situation in life, a particular person to deal with, a specific project or work problem. Passages and notes attach to a group.

## Finding passages

- Search and browse by category type, category, tag, keyword.
- AI situation matching (paid tier): free-text situation in, candidate passages out.
- Quick capture of a situation by keyboard or microphone through App Intents or snippets, so the ask can be made without opening the app.

## Delivery and habit

- Flash-card presentation.
- Daily reminders by scheduled notification, random or planned.
- Open: widgets, lock-screen or StandBy surfaces, Watch, chapter-of-the-day habit (the one habit people already have with this book).

## UX and UI

- Open to a variety of styles; no direction chosen.
- Reference points: the card deck's icon-per-trait back; the vault's [[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish]] note for flash-card feel if cards are used.

## From the card deck

- One verse per card, large type, reference below.
- A fixed set of seven traits of a wise person on every card back, with the traits the verse exhibits marked. A passage can exhibit several traits.
- Cards are numbered, which suggests a canonical deck order separate from chapter order.

## Questions to settle before V1

- Text version and license.
- Unit of categorization and the category types.
- Whether categorization ships as fixed data or can grow.
- On-device versus hosted AI, and whether the free tier gets any AI at all.
- Which delivery surfaces are V1 and which are later.
