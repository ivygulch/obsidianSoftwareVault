# The Wisdom Deck — Reference

A physical card deck, "The Wisdom Deck", copyright 2008 Mark Matlock, that seeded part of the [[Applying Proverbs]] idea. Two cards photographed 2026-10-03 (cards 2 and 12).

## Design

**Front:** one verse in large white type on a dark textured ground, the reference above it ("PROVERBS 11:1"), the deck name and card number in the corners.

**Back:** seven icons with labels, the same seven on every card, laid out as a grid. The traits the verse exhibits are outlined with a box. The seven traits:

1. trusts in God
2. exercises self-control
3. walks in healthy relationships
4. keeps balance
5. seeks good counsel
6. manages resources
7. speaks carefully

**Card 2, Proverbs 11:1** — "The Lord abhors dishonest scales, but accurate weights are his delight." Boxed: seeks good counsel, manages resources.

**Card 12, Proverbs 14:31** — "He who oppresses the poor shows contempt for their Maker, but whoever is kind to the needy honors God." Boxed: trusts in God, exercises self-control, manages resources.

The wording matches the 1984 NIV.

## What it contributes to the idea

- A worked example of the "individual characteristics" category type: a small fixed vocabulary of traits, each passage marked with the ones it exhibits, many-to-many.
- Icons as the compact representation of a category, legible at card size.
- A canonical deck order (card numbers) independent of chapter order, which a flash-card mode could reuse.
- The verse-per-card unit, which does not handle multi-verse sayings; a digital version can.

## Open

- How many cards are in the full deck, and whether the whole book is covered or a selection.
- Whether the deck's trait assignments are reusable as data or only as a design precedent (the deck is copyrighted; the trait names are short common phrases, the assignments are the author's editorial work).
