# Volunteer Fire Department Coordinator — Notes

- **Funding**: paid by the department, not Jason personally. In Jason's words, "money isn't a problem."
- **Current system**: an old PC-based system, accessible only from the department PC and computers in some trucks. No tie to modern devices or remote access.

## 2026-08-25 — Scope confirmed: calendar only

The primary use case is the original ask from the department (via Jason's text thread), verbatim:

> Hello, are you interested in doing a calander app for our department only? Not something for broader audience. Private to us with monthly events, reminders, possible attendees status (committed, possible, can't make it) - accessible to members and editable from phone by admins

No need for records management or other advanced features. The fire-specific platforms (IamResponding, Responserack, Station Boss) are beyond this scope — kept in [[Existing Solutions]] for awareness only.
