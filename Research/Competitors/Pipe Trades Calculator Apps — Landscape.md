# Pipe Trades Calculator Apps — Landscape

> Deep dive from the [[Indie App Opportunity Scan — 2026-10-02]]. Store figures were fetched on 2026-10-02; every revenue figure is an estimate resting on an assumed ratings-to-downloads ratio. Keyword scores come from the appstore MCP's heuristic tool because the astro MCP was not connected.

Scope: iOS field calculators and data references for pipefitters, steamfitters and pipe welders, with machinist feeds-and-speeds as a secondary niche. Fetched 2026-10-02. Method: iTunes Search/Lookup API (US, GB, CA, AU), customer-reviews RSS (up to 100 most recent per app), Wayback snapshots of App Store pages for historical rating counts, Apple's search-hints endpoint, the appstore MCP for Google Play and keyword scores, and web search. The astro MCP refused the connection, so this report contains no Astro figures. Figures not directly fetched are marked (estimate) or (unverified). Rating count is a size proxy, not revenue.

Two findings change the Phase 2 picture. A current one-time-price rival now exists: PipeBook Pipefitter Calculator ($12.99, released 2026-08-06), positioned on the "one purchase, no subscription, offline" wedge, one of 23 pipe-trade apps released in 2026. And Pipe Trades Pro's rating count is growing faster under the subscription, not slower.

## 4. Size outside the store

- BLS: 504,500 plumbers, pipefitters and steamfitters employed in 2024, about 44,000 openings a year, median pay $62,970 (via search summary). The pipefitter/steamfitter share is not broken out (unverified).
- United Association: about 409,000 members in the US and Canada (sources range 355,000–412,000). Training involves "approximately 100,000 journeymen and apprentices in over 400 local training facilities"; one source puts apprentices near 12% of membership, about 49,000 (estimate).
- Communities: Moes Math (Mark Duewerth, Local 597, named in a Pipe Trades Pro review) has 6.76K YouTube subscribers; most pipefitting-math channels are under 1K. Subreddit sizes could not be fetched.
- Tool spend: fitters buy their own hand tools (one cited agreement reimburses half of initial cost up to $4,500). Reviewers' reference points are the $35–$70 calculator and the $28–$46 Blue Book.

## 5. Monetization evidence

What is charged: $4.99–$19.99 one-time; $39.99/yr at Pipe Trades Pro; QuickBend $6.99, raised from $3.99 after 2024-02.

Rating accrual from Wayback snapshots (measured):

| App | Snapshots | Ratings per year |
|---|---|---|
| QuickBend | 594 (2019-10) → 1,364 (2021-10) → 2,313 (2024-02) → 3,969 (2026-10) | 383 → 418 → 622 |
| Pipe Trades Pro | 3,251 (2025-02) → 3,734 (2026-01) → 4,330 (2026-10) | 519 → 846 |

Pipe Trades Pro is adding ratings faster than QuickBend while subscription-only, and a reviewer notes it "demanded a review." No one-time pipe app has come close: Piping DataBase 214 in 16 years, Pipe Calc Elite about 11 a year, Pipe Fitter Calculator about 2 a year.

Chart rank is weak evidence. QuickBend at #1 Productivity paid is real, but the same day's charts carry Pipe Fitting 33 ($0.99, 1 rating) at #22 Business paid and Pipe Fitter Calculator (35 ratings in 14 years) at #33 Productivity paid.

Revenue range for a competent entrant — estimate. Assumptions: one-time $9.99, net $8.49 after 15%; one rating per 20 buyers (assumed, band 10–30, no source); iOS US only.

- QuickBend calibration: 622 ratings/yr × 20 = 12,440 sales × $5.94 net = about $74K/yr (band $37K–$111K).
- Low: 5 or fewer ratings a year, where 20 of the 23 entrants of 2026 and the stale apps sit. 100 sales × $8.49 = about $850/yr, under $100/month.
- Mid: 40–100 ratings a year (PipeBook's first eight weeks annualise to about 65; sustained pace unverified). 800–2,000 sales × $8.49 = $6.8K–$17K/yr, about $570–$1,400/month. This brackets the $300–$850/month pay-once floor in p1-revenue.md.
- High: 300–600 ratings a year, QuickBend's and Pipe Trades Pro's range. 6,000–12,000 sales × $8.49 = $51K–$102K/yr. QuickBend needed four years to reach 594 ratings.

One-time against a low annual price, per payer: $12.99 one-time nets $11.04 once. $9.99/yr nets $8.49 a year; with the calibration note's first renewal of 23–40% and third renewal of 56–70% (second interpolated at 45–60%), four-year value is $8.49 × 1.39 to 1.81 = $11.81–$15.35. The annual plan breaks even with $12.99 one-time only if it converts 72–93% as many buyers, in an audience whose reviews refuse subscriptions for a calculator, including one at $5/month.

## 6. Build scope

Smallest version that beats incumbents on the stated complaints: one-time purchase; fully offline with no entitlement check; feet-inch-fraction entry with backspace; simple, rolling and parallel offsets returning travel and cut length net of take-offs and weld gap; odd-angle elbow cuts and miters; tables for pipe schedules, butt-weld take-offs and flanges with stud count, length and ring number; 48 inch and larger; metric switch; iPad and landscape. PipeBook already ships most of this. Requested and not covered by any current app: Victaulic grooved and threaded/socket-weld take-offs with thread makeup, saddle/lateral/fish-mouth layouts, 3R/5D bends, large flanges, line blanks.

Data sources: ASME B36.10M/B36.19M (pipe), B16.9 (fittings), B16.5/B16.47 (flanges), B16.11 (socket-weld/threaded), B16.20 (gaskets), B16.48 (line blanks); manufacturer catalogs (Weldbend, Bonney Forge, Victaulic).

Licensing, stated plainly: ASME claims copyright in its standards and requires written permission (ip@asme.org) to republish excerpts, including in software. Individual dimensions are facts, and US law gives factual compilations only thin protection for selection and arrangement; that is a legal position, not a clearance, and no legal opinion was obtained. In practice the tables are republished by manufacturers, free sites (flangeboltchart.com, troopindustrial.com) and every app above; PipeBook describes extracting each value from two published sources and checking a third. Whether any incumbent holds an ASME licence is unverified. Derivable without a table: all offset and miter trigonometry; odd-angle take-off as tan(θ/2) × radius; pipe weight and fill from OD and wall; long-radius 90 centre-to-end as 1.5 × nominal size. Not derivable: ODs, wall thicknesses, flange and small-size fitting dimensions.

Frameworks: SwiftUI, StoreKit 2, bundled tables, Canvas diagrams, a custom fraction type and keypad, unit tests against worked examples. No backend or account.

To learn, and from where: the Pipe Fitters Blue Book; the Pipe Trades Pro 4095 user's guide (free PDF, a worked example per function); Moes Math videos; a working fitter to check output.

Yearly upkeep (estimate): one iOS-release pass, table corrections from user reports, occasional standards revisions (cadence unverified), email support. Easy Offset shows the cost of skipping it.

## 7. Risks and strikes

- Content treadmill, network effect, backend: none.
- Support: accuracy disputes; one wrong value damages trust in the whole table set.
- Licence dependence: moderate and unresolved (section 6).
- Hardware: none needed, but phone bans at refineries and plants (two reviewers) keep the handheld in use and cap the addressable share by an unknown amount.
- Regulated liability: none; a wrong dimension costs material and rework. LMF Services disclaims: "Users are responsible for verifying all measurements and dimensions before fabrication ... The developer assumes no responsibility for errors."
- Absorption by an AI assistant: the trigonometry is trivial for one; offline use and trusted tables are not. The visible effect is on supply: 23 new pipe apps in 2026 (attribution to AI-assisted building is inference).
- Calculated Industries adds a one-time price: it sold one at about $25 until 2022, has held the subscription through four and a half years of complaints while ratings accelerate, and owns the training-center channel. A one-time tier would remove the wedge; no sign of one (unverified).
- A stale seller waking: already happening. LMF Services updated the Android Pipe Fitter Calculator on 2026-09-28; Cyberprodigy (Pipe Calc Elite) updated its conduit app 2026-09-06.
- Wedge already occupied: PipeBook holds the positioning at $12.99 with an active solo developer; ten ratings in eight weeks.
- Discovery: twenty 2026 entrants sit at 0–3 ratings.

## 8. Reach

- App Store search: autocomplete confirms intent (section 3); results below Pipe Trades Pro are thin, with new titles arriving monthly.
- UA locals and training centers: the channel that made Pipe Trades Pro standard. Access for an outsider is unverified.
- YouTube: Moes Math 6.76K subscribers; larger piping channels (construction L&i 247K) are not US-union focused.
- Word of mouth on the job, the recurring story in QuickBend reviews ("my JW literally just told me about this today").
- Reddit and Facebook groups: sizes and self-promotion rules not verified.

## 9. Machinist sub-niche

Market: CNC Machinist Calculator Pro (Shane Anderson) $14.99, 678 ratings, 4.72, released 2015-09-25, updated 2024-10-05, absent from every saved top-paid chart. The same seller's successor, CNC Machinist Calculator Ultra (free + IAP, prices not fetched), has 26 ratings after 17 months, updated 2026-09-10. FSWizard PRO $17.99 has 24; Calculated Industries' Machinist Calc Pro 2, $29.99, has 5. Free vendor apps hold comparable counts: The Phillips Machinist 393, Machining Advisor Pro 163, MyHaas 151.

Pay and weakness: the Pro app's last 50 reviews run 34/7/4/1/4 at about eight a year; 3★ 2025-03-06 "the creators of it seem to have abandoned it". Desktop tools charge more (G-Wizard $79/yr; HSMAdvisor $65–$219, Phase 1). Headcount: about 299,500 machinists and 176,950 CNC tool operators (BLS, Phase 1).

Second product on the same engine: the shell carries over — fraction and unit numeric type, keypad, table browser, diagrams, purchase flow. The content does not: feeds and speeds rest on a material-by-tool coefficient database, plus thread, drill and tap tables. Proven store demand is about one-sixth of Pipe Trades Pro's by ratings, and the largest paid app's own seller has not moved its users to the successor.
