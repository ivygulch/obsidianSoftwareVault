> Pillar report for [[Mobile Card Games — Landscape]]. Monetization, retention, AI fairness, multiplayer infra, localization, IP, platform features, session design. Data pulled 2026-09-10 (US App Store via the appstore MCP; web sources cited inline).

# Mobile Card Games — Other Areas


Scope: a premium, no-ads golf-family card game (Skyjo-style) with solo vs AI, pass-and-play, async online, and a daily seeded challenge. Findings are cited; items marked **[unverified]** rest on a single secondary source or a search summary I could not confirm against a primary page.

## 1. Monetization patterns and reception

**Premium buy-once is viable again, but the winners are PC-proven brands.** AppMagic data reported via Pocket Tactics: 2025 saw a 77% increase in premium titles on iOS/Android (nearly 750), and premium is still only 4% of downloads. Balatro mobile: 3.1M downloads, $21.3M revenue at $9.99; Slay the Spire $13.7M; Dead Cells $6.5M. Balatro did about $1M in its first week and peaked at $139.7K/day. (https://www.pockettactics.com/premium-mobile-games-increase, https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-approaches-1-million-in-seven-days-on-mobile/, https://toucharcade.com/2024/09/05/balatro-mobile-release-date-price-download-apple-arcade/)

**Price points observed on iOS (verified via App Store data unless noted):** Balatro $9.99; Slay the Spire $9.99 (sale floor $6.99, https://www.cheapcharts.com/us/itunes/apps/1491530147/Slay-the-Spire); Exploding Kittens 2 $4.99 paid, no IAP items surfaced by the store API; Card Thief / Card Crawl (Tinytouchtales, two-person studio) $2.99 each with lifetime revenue of roughly 38K EUR and 33K EUR respectively per the studio's own site, and their free-with-unlock Card Crawl Adventure at about 12K EUR **[unverified: figures from search summary of tinytouchtales.com]** (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1907000/ios-tinytouchtales-sale-card-thief-card-crawl-0-do, http://www.tinytouchtales.com/).

**Expectation problem in paid apps:** Exploding Kittens 2 reviews complain that after paying $5 the app asks $18 for expansions "each costing more than the entire game," plus the Steam-to-mobile DLC rebuy issue. Paid-plus-IAP reads as aggressive when the base is premium. (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/exploding-kittens-2/id6478287619?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone, https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/games/exploding-kittens-2)

**Subscription for "no ads":** MobilityWare Solitaire charges $1.99/month, $4.99/3 months, $14.99/year for ad removal, daily challenges and four themes. Reviews explicitly say "I refuse to pay a monthly subscription, a one-time fee would be preferred." (https://www.solitairegameguide.com/reviews/mobilityware-solitaire-review, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solitaire-by-mobilityware/id284791396?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)

**Apple Arcade:** Card titles on Arcade include Balatro+, Solitaire Stories, Solitaire by MobilityWare+, UNO: Arcade Edition, Crazy Eights+, Gin Rummy Classic+, Spades+, Hearts+, Spider Solitaire+, Flipflop Solitaire+ **[unverified list; from Pocket Gamer search summary]** (https://www.pocketgamer.com/apple-arcade/list-of-every-apple-arcade-game/). Arcade economics: upfront fees have shrunk since 2020; "+" re-releases of existing titles receive no upfront payment, only bonus-pool money keyed to an undefined "qualifying session" metric that favors long-retention games and disadvantages short premium indies (https://mobilegamer.biz/inside-apple-arcade-axed-games-declining-payouts-disillusioned-studios-and-an-uncertain-future/). Arcade exclusivity blocks other mobile stores but allows console/PC (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Arcade).

**Free-to-play comparators in the golf family:** Play Nine is free with a coin economy; reviews say 500 coins are needed per multiplayer game, an ad runs every hole, and 15 minutes of play earns 100 coins. The Golf Card Game (Glowing Eye) and Sky-jo (Anusha) list as free with no IAP items visible to the store API, i.e., ad-supported; Sky-jo reviews say players "would happily pay for the app to avoid having to watch ads after each game." (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/play-nine-golf-card-game/id1567060312?see-all=reviews, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sky-jo-golf-card-game/id1567186753)

**So what:** The $4.99–$9.99 band is populated by PC-proven titles with brand pull; $2.99 is the observed indie band and yields tens of thousands of euros lifetime, not hundreds. Every golf-family competitor is ad-supported and their reviews contain an explicit unmet "let me pay once" demand.

## 2. Retention mechanics without ads or coins

**Daily streaks are the top return driver.** Statista's 2025 US survey lists daily streak/engagement bonuses as the main factor bringing mobile players back, ahead of in-game events (https://www.statista.com/statistics/1614803/main-factors-for-us-mobile-gamers-to-return-to-favorite-games). Marvel Snap's daily loop (missions, login tokens, mission tracks feeding the season) is a circuit "most players run through in just a few minutes every day"; average D1 retention across mobile was about 27% in 2025 and roughly 75% of games are under 3% by D28 (https://marvelsnapzone.com/daily-reward-loops-work-the-same-way-in-card-games-and-sweepstakes-slots/, https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2023/5/23/marvel-snap-the-definitive-deconstruction).

**Seeded content is a solved pattern.** Balatro exposes seed codes; community sites exist purely to share and analyze seeds. Balatro marks seeded runs so they do not feed unlocks, separating "practice/share" from progression. A 2025 patch changed seed generation for Gold Stake, which shifted shared-seed outcomes, so seed stability across versions is a real compatibility concern (https://balatrohq.com/seeds/, https://balatroseed.net/, https://balatrowiki.org/w/Updates). Solitaire-of-the-day sites deliberately copy the Wordle "same puzzle for everyone" format (https://cardgames.io/solitaire-of-the-day/). Solitaire Palace competes live on identical setups so results are comparable (https://www.solitaire-palace.com/).

**Stats screens as a trust and retention feature.** Cribbage Pro tracks every game (wins, losses, completion, high hands, pegging) and publishes that even top players only win about 60% against weaker opponents (https://www.cribbagepro.net/about/cribbage-pro-statistics.html). Just Cribbage shows smoothed "Rank Points" rather than raw Glicko-2 because raw ratings move counterintuitively (https://just-cribbage.web.app/).

**Platform-provided loops (iOS 26).** The Games app surfaces Game Center achievements, leaderboards, and friend activity, and lets players create time-limited Challenges (1, 3, or 7 days) on any game with a Game Center leaderboard; friends are notified when a score is beaten. In-App Events appear in both the Games app and the App Store (https://developer.apple.com/games-app/, https://support.apple.com/en-mide/guide/iphone/iph9ce5a2dee/26/ios/26, https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-26-games-app/).

**So what:** A seeded daily with a shareable code and a Game Center leaderboard gets a free Challenges surface in the iOS 26 Games app, which is the closest thing to a system-level retention loop available to a premium game. Stats-per-AI-persona and streaks cost nothing to run and are what players in this genre cite as reasons to return.

## 3. AI opponents, fairness perception, and bot honesty

**The "rigged" accusation arrives regardless of implementation.** Cribbage Pro's developer documented a surge of cheating accusations coinciding with a download spike, noting that writing cheating code would have taken longer than writing a competent AI, and that player win stats showed the opposite (https://blog.cribbagepro.net/2010/01/hey-your-game-cheats.html). Microsoft Solitaire and Solitaire Till Dawn field the same complaints; analyses attribute it to pattern-seeking bias, while noting some lesser apps showed suspicious patterns near IAP prompts (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/5519026/microsoft-solitaire-game-is-rigged-why, https://www.solitairegameguide.com/blog/solitaire-rng-science, http://solitairetilldawn.blogspot.com/2014/11/are-shuffles-random.html). In the golf family specifically, Play Nine reviews say "the lack of a true random shuffle" and that losing drains coins so the developer profits (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/play-nine-golf-card-game/id1567060312?see-all=reviews).

**Mitigations that ship:** Cribbage Pro publishes shuffle audits (card-position frequency over large samples) (https://blog.cribbagepro.net/2019/01/updated-deck-shuffling-and-randomness.html). Just Cribbage goes further with commit-reveal: SHA-256 hash of the seed shown before the deal, seed revealed after, Fisher-Yates over a SHA-256 stream so anyone can reproduce the deck, plus an automated test proving the AI's decisions do not change when hidden cards are swapped, and difficulty adjusts decision quality only, never information access (https://just-cribbage.web.app/).

**Difficulty and personality presentation:** Euchre 3D ships AI with "unique play styles, playful chatting, and distinct personalities" and an Expert level; NeuralPlay's Euchre uses six levels from "I'm still figuring this out" to "seasoned card shark" and Monte Carlo simulation at high levels; the developer exposes an "AI bidding aggressiveness" setting after complaints (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/euchre-3d/id364909474, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.neuralplay.android.euchre&hl=en_US). Euchre 3D reviews still complain the AI "consistently gets better hands," so personality does not remove the perception problem.

**Bots posing as humans is a named grievance.** UNO! reviews: "the game is full of bots," suspected bots never accept friend requests and have generic names; a Google Play developer-community thread accuses UNO of using bots to cheat. Play Nine reviews say players with generic first-name usernames get "3 or more -5 cards every round." Trickster fills empty seats with bots by design and one review says "the bots are useless, I exit games when bots appear." (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/uno/id1344700142?see-all=reviews&platform=mac, https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/thread/326444560/, https://www.trickstercards.com/faq/)

**So what:** In this genre the fairness complaint is a certainty, not a risk, so the cheap defenses (visible seed, per-persona win-rate stats, "AI never sees your face-down cards" statement) are worth shipping at launch. Never seat a bot as a human; label it, and let solo-vs-AI be honest about being solo.

## 4. Multiplayer infrastructure for a small team

**Game Center turn-based (GKTurnBasedMatch):** store-and-forward async, 2–16 players, invites via friends/contacts/automatch, no server to run (https://developer.apple.com/documentation/GameKit/creating-turn-based-games). It is Apple-only, which rules it out if Android is a requirement (https://developer.apple.com/game-center/). Developer-forum history shows matches vanishing (about one in six in one report, mass disappearance after an app update in another) and turn notifications arriving without match data; these are old threads and I found no 2025-specific reliability reports **[unverified for current iOS]** (https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/36625, https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/670561, https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/26441).

**Custom backends:** Firebase is repeatedly recommended for small-team turn-based games (turn resolution, matchmaking metadata, progression, push); Supabase for teams wanting Postgres and less lock-in; Nakama offers true game-server features but "demands more DevOps and backend engineering effort" (https://namazustudios.com/best-real-time-game-backends/, https://encore.dev/articles/supabase-alternatives, https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Nakama-Game-Server/alternatives).

**What shipped card games actually do and what breaks:** Trickster uses email/password accounts, 5-character join codes and share links, cross-platform, with stats synced across devices (https://www.trickstercards.com/features/join-friends/). Play Nine uses share-by-code private games and public matchmaking with in-game chat (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/play-nine-golf-card-game/id1567060312). Carcassonne (Asmodee) has cross-platform async with no time limit in friends games and a countdown clock in Quick Play; abandonment is handled by letting the remaining player play the absent opponent's turns, and one reviewer reports roughly one in four multiplayer matches "breaks and boots one of the players" (https://www.pixelatedcardboard.com/carcassonne-asmodee-review/, https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/carcassonne-android-app/). Star Realms' async history is dominated by push-notification complaints; players specifically want the push to open the match, not a lobby, and the ongoing-games list leaked results ("spoilers") (https://www.starrealms.com/itunes-notifications-fixed/, https://videogamegeek.com/thread/1252398/ios-notifications). Ascension reviews cite Android notification failures, no private rooms or friend invites online, and purchased expansions lost on an update (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playdekgames.android.Ascension). Cribbage Pro's real-time mode enforces 10-second turn limits with two warnings then forfeit, records quits as losses, and adds a five-minute lockout after disconnects (https://blog.cribbagepro.net/2019/10/multiplayer-points-system-2019-revisions.html).

**SharePlay:** I found no evidence of card games adopting GroupActivities in a way that reviews or press mention; treat as low value **[unverified negative]**.

**So what:** The review corpus for async card games is dominated by three failures: pushes that do not deep-link into the match, abandoned opponents, and lost match state. Game Center covers iOS-only async with zero server cost but has a documented history of match loss and blocks Android; a Firebase-class backend with join codes is what every cross-platform peer actually uses.

## 5. Localization, regional markets, featuring, ASO

**France is the Skyjo market.** French Wikipedia: over 3M copies sold in France by 2024, on the Christmas 2024 volume podium, and number one at JouéClub in 2023 and 2024; distributed in France by Blackrock Games (https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyjo, https://fr.accio.com/business/skyjo-jeu-le-plus-vendu). Germany is the home market (Magilano, Hachenburg) and one of the largest card-game markets by value with the Spiel des Jahres award driving mainstream awareness (https://magilano.com/en, https://www.indexbox.io/store/germany-kw-card-game-840-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/). The Skyjo box ships in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian (https://www.skyjo.info/).

**Clones already localize store listings per storefront.** The same app is titled "Skyjo-o: Flip your Skylza" (US), "skyjo-o: Dreh dein Skylza um" (DE), and "skyjo-o : retourne ton skyjoe" (FR). German-store search for "skyjo" returns two "Punkteblock" score-keeper apps, a "Begleiter" companion, and a "Punktezähler", i.e., a German-language utility ecosystem exists around the physical game (App Store search, DE storefront, 2026-09-10).

**Phase 10 as the European rummy comparator:** 17M Android installs, 110K in the last 30 days, 4.72 rating on 570K ratings, ranked 7 in Card on Play **[unverified: AppBrain estimates]** (https://www.appbrain.com/app/phase-10-casual-card-game/com.mattel163.phase10).

**Featuring:** Nominations go through App Store Connect (App Launch, App Enhancement, New Content) at least three weeks ahead; editors score on user experience, UI design, innovation, uniqueness, accessibility, localization, and product-page quality, and evaluate the product page first (https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/how-to-get-your-app-featured-on-the-app-store, https://appscreenshotstudio.com/blog/get-featured-on-the-app-store-2026-nominations-guide). Recent "Games We Love" card picks are animated card battlers and story deck-builders (Foretales), not classic adaptations (https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/story/id1856287604, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/foretales/id6743491591).

**ASO:** I could not find a public 2025 keyword-difficulty report for the Card category **[gap]**. Observationally, "golf card game" on the US store returns eleven apps, all free, from Glowing Eye (6,451 ratings), Play Nine, and a tail of 2025-dated one-person apps; "skyjo" returns Sky-jo (3,269 ratings, released 2021, no IAP listed), Skyjo-o (29 ratings, 2025), Skygo, and score keepers (App Store search, 2026-09-10).

**So what:** French and German localization are not nice-to-haves for a Skyjo-family game; France alone has sold 3M physical copies and the clones already localize their titles per storefront. The "skyjo" search term is owned by a 2021 ad-supported clone and a cluster of score-keepers, so brand-term ranking is winnable but only with the mark's cooperation (section 6).

## 6. Legal and IP dynamics

**SKYJO is a registered US trademark** (Reg. 7536287, owner Alexander Bernhardt, Hachenburg; filed 2023-11-20, registered 2024-10-15, first use in commerce 2016-08-10, class: toys and sporting goods) (https://www.trademarkia.com/skyjo-98278593). Whether the registration covers software/games is not shown in the summary **[unverified]**.

**Clones sit on the term today.** The US store hosts "Sky-jo: Golf Card Game" (Anusha s t, since 2021), "Skyjo-o", "Skygo", and score keepers, none from Magilano; I found no Magilano-published app (App Store search; https://magilano.com/en). The Skyjo-o listing avoids the plain word in its title ("Skylza", "skyjoe", "sky-joe") while keeping "skyjo" in the bundle ID and copy, a pattern consistent with a developer working around a name dispute **[inference]** (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skyjo-o-flip-your-skylza/id6744247458).

**Takedown mechanics:** Apple's App Name Dispute and IP dispute forms route the claim to the developer and ask both parties to resolve it directly; Apple does not adjudicate (https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/dispute-forms/app-store/, https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/dispute-forms/app-store/app-name-dispute.html). Precedents: Ravensburger's "Memory" mark triggered takedown notices against multiple iOS apps using the word; Catan's lawyers pursued "Island Settlers" for merely mentioning Catan (https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/-memory-board-game-trademark-threatens-several-ios-games, https://www.techdirt.com/2011/02/22/how-lawyers-settlers-catan-abuse-ip-law-to-take-down-perfectly-legal-competitors/). Trademark, not copyright, is what blocks a clone written from scratch because it works on likelihood of confusion (https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/protect-app-from-copycats, https://brandr.legal/en/how-copyright-registration-for-a-mobile-app-helps-block-clones/).

**How official apps signal it:** Mattel163 uses the ™ in titles ("Skip-Bo™"), "from the creators of UNO and Phase 10" in copy, and the Mattel publisher name (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skip-bo-solitaire-card-game/id1538912205).

**So what:** Rules are not protectable but the SKYJO word mark is registered and actively enforced elsewhere by the board-game industry, so a generic-named golf game is safe and an officially licensed one is the only way to rank on the brand term; either way the current clone occupant has a four-year head start on ratings.

## 7. Platform features card games exploit

**iOS 26 Games app** is the one new surface with proven mechanics for this genre: Challenges built on Game Center leaderboards, friend-beat notifications, In-App Events shown in the Games app and App Store, recommendations from what friends play (https://developer.apple.com/games-app/, https://9to5mac.com/2025/11/12/ios-26-2s-apple-games-app-has-three-new-improvements/).

**Live Activities / Dynamic Island** run on iOS 16.1+ via ActivityKit in a WidgetKit extension, update from the app or via APNs, and a small team can ship one in one to two weeks; behavior must be validated on hardware, not the simulator (https://newly.app/guides/ios-live-activities). I found no shipped turn-based card game advertising a "your turn" Live Activity **[unverified negative]**; Words With Friends' 2024–2025 updates were single-player modes and year-in-review, not Live Activities (https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/words-friends-launches-new-suite-game-modes).

**Widgets / StandBy:** No card game with a documented daily-challenge widget surfaced; NYT Games does not appear to ship a lock-screen widget either **[unverified negative]** (https://newly.app/articles/ios-widgets).

**Apple Watch:** a small ecosystem of solitaire apps exists (Klondike with Daily Challenges, Solitaire Time with crown control), with reviews noting tap precision at watch-face edges; nothing multiplayer (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/klondike-solitaire-game-watch/id6473575512, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solitaire-time-classic-solitaire-anywhere/id1035168423).

**Mac/iPad:** Balatro and Slay the Spire ship as Arcade "+" titles across iPhone, iPad, Mac; the premium Balatro is iPhone/iPad. Controller support is a Games-app browse facet but irrelevant to a tap-to-flip card game.

**So what:** Only two platform features have a demonstrated payoff for a card game: Game Center leaderboards (because iOS 26 Challenges are built on them) and push-to-match deep links for async. Live Activities for "your turn" are cheap and unoccupied by competitors, so they are a differentiator rather than table stakes.

**Apple TV / tvOS presence (checked 2026-09-10, App Store Compatibility sections).** Among the top titles, only three have a tvOS build: MobilityWare's free Solitaire (tvOS 9.0+, the only ad-supported one), Balatro+ (Arcade only, tvOS 13.4+, controller support; the $9.99 premium Balatro is iPhone/iPad/Vision only), and UNO: Arcade Edition (Arcade only, tvOS 17.0+, controllers, SharePlay). Solitaire Stories (Red Games, Arcade) also runs on tvOS 13.0+. No tvOS build: UNO!, Phase 10, Skip-Bo, Solitaire Grand Harvest, MobilityWare+ (Arcade version, oddly), Marvel Snap, Hearthstone, Pokémon TCG Pocket, Slay the Spire, Play Nine, Exploding Kittens 2, Trickster, Spades Plus, Euchre 3D, Cribbage Pro. Apple's "Popular Apple Arcade Games for TV" room lists 20 titles of which two are card games (Balatro+, Solitaire by MobilityWare+). The tvOS store's native card offering is thin: "Solitaire - TV" (solo dev, 2021, too few ratings to show a score). Mac Catalyst / Apple Silicon Mac support is far more common than tvOS (UNO!, Marvel Snap, Play Nine, Exploding Kittens 2, Euchre 3D, MobilityWare free). Android TV: Google Play Games for Android TV ships a built-in solitaire; Odesys publishes solitaire on Fire TV; none of the top card titles advertise Android TV or Fire TV builds **[unverified negative]**. Sources: the App Store product pages for each title; https://apps.apple.com/us/tv/room/1485306153; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solitaire-tv/id1581329395; https://www.apkmirror.com/apk/google-inc/google-play-games-android-tv/.

## 8. Session design

**Orientation:** Golf-family and solitaire apps default to portrait with an optional landscape mode; MobilityWare added landscape because cards "may appear small" in portrait, and one-handed solitaire titles are portrait-only with left/right-hand modes (https://mobilityware.helpshift.com/hc/en/10-solitaire/faq/507-how-do-i-play-in-landscape-orientation-1579115589/, https://minireview.io/card/one-handed-solitaire). Balatro mobile is landscape-only and the community built a portrait mod with one-handed left/right settings and swipe-to-play gestures, which is direct evidence of demand for portrait in a card game (https://github.com/ShaggyLorean/balatro-portrait-mobile).

**Session length is user-set in this genre.** The Golf Card Game lets players pick 3, 6, or 9 rounds "because sometimes you'll just want a quick game"; Play Nine lets players choose 2–9 holes and 2–4 players (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-golf-card-game/id1114415982, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/play-nine-golf-card-game/id1567060312). Cribbage Pro's real-time online play uses 10-second turn timers with forfeit, i.e., a different session contract than async (https://blog.cribbagepro.net/2019/10/multiplayer-points-system-2019-revisions.html).

**Interruption and resume:** Apple's guidance is to save state "quickly and precisely so people can seamlessly continue where they left off" and to resume game audio after interruptions (https://codershigh.github.io/guidelines/ios/human-interface-guidelines/features/multitasking/index.html, https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Audio/Conceptual/AudioSessionProgrammingGuide/HandlingAudioInterruptions/HandlingAudioInterruptions.html). Abandoned online games are the async analogue; see section 4 for how Carcassonne and Cribbage Pro handle it.

**Offline-first:** every golf-family competitor advertises offline AI play as a headline feature ("unlimited free gameplay" offline in Play Nine; "Play against the AI offline" in Skyjo-o; "Play Offline" in Sky-jo).

**So what:** The genre's session contract is "portrait, one thumb, a round in under two minutes, and I choose how many rounds"; Balatro's landscape-only choice generated a portrait mod, which is the clearest signal that portrait is the default expectation. Offline solo is table stakes in every listing, so the premium pitch has to rest on what the free apps do badly: no ads, honest AI, and async that resumes cleanly.
