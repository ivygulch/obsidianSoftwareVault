# Mobile Card Games — Feature Priorities

## Tier 1 — Trust and honesty (the universal complaint stack)

1. **Provably fair dealing.** Commit-reveal seed (hash shown before the deal, seed revealed after), published shuffle statistics, and a stated and tested guarantee that the AI never reads face-down cards. Rigged-dealing is the top complaint in 14 of 20 apps including Balatro at $9.99; Cribbage Pro and Just Cribbage show the mitigations. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] a1, [[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §3)
2. **Legible, skillful AI with tiered difficulty.** Bots that actually try to go out, a visible thinking pause scaled to decision difficulty, and a post-round "what were they thinking" view. Dumb or unreadable AI is the top gameplay complaint in every golf-family app (Sky-jo, Skygo, Play Nine, Golf Card Game). ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] a5, [[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §1)
3. **No bots posing as humans.** Label every bot; keep solo honest. Named grievance in 7 apps; MobilityWare's fake chat and Spades Plus's human-ID bots are 1-star triggers. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] a4, d3)
4. **One-time price, no ads, no subscription, and the purchase never degrades.** Users quote $5–10; Euchre 3D's move to a $9.99/month ad-removal sub produced a 54% recent 1-star rate on a 4.82 app; Five Crowns added ads after an ad-free launch. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] c, d1, d8; [[Mobile Card Games — Market|Market]])

## Tier 2 — Fidelity and readability

5. **Faithful rules and official card colors, numerals always shown, color-independent encoding.** Rule deviations are noticed by exactly the customers who own the box (6 apps); Sky-jo reviewers list the exact color bands; themed decks are liked until rank legibility suffers (Solitaire Stories); Balatro's High Contrast Cards is praised. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] a7, [[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]] obs. 7)
6. **Portrait, one-thumb layout with cards that never shrink.** Commit targets in the bottom third, nothing at the top edge, generous drop tolerance, hand not under the home indicator. Portrait is the most repeated request in landscape ports and a stated purchase reason in Dawncaster and Card Crawl; Sakamoto's "never scale cards down" rule. ([[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]] obs. 2, 6; [[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §1)
7. **Text scaling, VoiceOver grid, Reduce Motion crossfades.** "Now your cards are too small" and "a game for ants" are 1-star lines; a blind King's Corner reviewer praises full VoiceOver play; Balatro's partial Reduce Motion is criticized. ([[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §4, [[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]])

## Tier 3 — Core interaction polish

8. **Undo within the turn and peek-before-commit.** Undo/hint/replay is the most repeated UI praise across the set; Reigns' hold-to-preview and Fun Bridge's tap-to-explain are the praised commit patterns; mis-grab without undo is the complaint in King's Corner and Trickster. ([[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]] obs. 1, 4)
9. **Animation speed setting and one skippable celebration; animation never gates input.** Praised when short (Euchre 3D, Runeterra, Card Thief), criticized when it blocks a button (Marvel Snap, Exploding Kittens 2, Hearthstone menus). ([[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]] obs. 3, [[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §3)
10. **One physical motion vocabulary.** Overshoot easing, random-stagger deal, 1:1 drag with tilt, snap-and-settle, layered slide/snap/flip sounds, haptic transients, feedback proportional to card value, digit roll on scores. Balatro's Apple Design Award and Hearthstone's board are the reference cases. ([[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §3 catalog)
11. **Opponent turns authored to a rhythm, and a last-moves replay on return at that same pace.** Hearthstone sound team; Board Game Arena's replay button. ([[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §1–2)

## Tier 4 — Modes and social

12. **Async play done right: join codes, push that deep-links into the match, in-app inbox, abandoned-opponent handling, cross-platform accounts.** Playing with distant family is the top praise theme in 9 apps; async reviews fail on lobby-not-match pushes, abandonment, and lost state. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] b1, [[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §4)
13. **Pass-and-play with a proper handoff screen.** Requested in Exploding Kittens 2; no app in the set documents a good handoff. ([[Mobile Card Games — UI & Polish|UI & Polish]] §2)
14. **User-chosen round count, sub-two-minute rounds, offline solo.** The genre's session contract in every listing. ([[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §8)
15. **Learning aids and stats: post-round decision grading, per-persona win rates, hints that are never rationed.** Cribbage Pro's grading is the named teaching praise; rationing hints behind ads is resented; older players cite "keeps the brain sharp" and are the ones who pay. ([[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]], [[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] b6–7)

## Tier 5 — Retention and platform

16. **Seeded daily challenge with a shareable code and a Game Center leaderboard.** Streaks are the top return driver; iOS 26 Games app Challenges ride on leaderboards for free; no golf-family clone has one. ([[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §2, §7)
17. **House-rule variants.** Requested in Play Nine and Golf Card Game reviews; praised in Trickster and UNO. ([[Mobile Card Games — Reviews|Reviews]] a13, b8)
18. **"Your turn" Live Activity and widget.** Cheap and unoccupied by any shipped card game. ([[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §7)
19. **DE and FR at launch with per-storefront titles.** France is the largest Skyjo market; clones already localize titles. ([[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §5)
20. **Video or SharePlay at the table.** Trickster's video feature earns "next best thing to the same table"; no evidence of SharePlay adoption in card games. ([[Mobile Card Games — UX Praise|UX Praise]], [[Mobile Card Games — Other Areas|Other Areas]] §4)
