> Pillar report for [[Mobile Card Games — Landscape]]. Review mining across 20 apps with verbatim quotes and cross-app synthesis. Data pulled 2026-09-10 (US App Store via the appstore MCP; web sources cited inline).

# Mobile Card Games — Reviews


Method: per app, lifetime score and rating count; star distribution of the 100 newest reviews (recency signal, not lifetime); 40 newest plus 40 most-helpful reviews read. Theme counts approximate per ~80 reviews. Quotes verbatim, trimmed.

Pattern across every tier: lifetime scores of 4.4–4.9 (buoyed by rating prompts) sit on recent-100 distributions that are bimodal or majority 1-star.

## Synthesis

### (a) Complaint themes recurring across tiers (ranked by number of apps)

1. **Rigged or scripted dealing, AI that "gets exactly the card it needs"** — 14 apps: every F2P and folk app, the brand/clone apps with a bot, and Balatro at $9.99. In F2P it is read as a coin-sales tactic; in premium as bad RNG.
2. **Ads: unclosable, mid-hand, over silent mode, fake "update" ads, cross-promo pop-ups** — 12 apps. Most acute where ads interrupt a live multiplayer hand (Euchre 3D: kicked for AFK while an ad plays).
3. **Coin, energy, or wager economies where fee roughly equals payout** — 8 apps: UNO, Phase 10, Skip-Bo, Grand Harvest, Spades Plus, Trickster, Play Nine, Sky-jo.
4. **Bots masquerading as humans** — 7 apps: UNO, Skip-Bo, MobilityWare, Snap, Spades Plus, Trickster, Play Nine. Detected by inhuman speed, sabotage plays, or a fake chat feature.
5. **Dumb AI partner or opponent (not rigged, just bad)** — 7 apps: Trickster, Spades Plus, Euchre 3D, Play Nine, Sky-jo, Skygo, Golf Card Game. Bots that don't try to go out, bid on nothing, "no regard to zipping."
6. **Crashes or regressions right after an update** — 7 apps: Phase 10, Snap, Hearthstone, Balatro, Sky-jo, Five Crowns, DEKÜ.
7. **Rule fidelity to the physical game** — 6 apps: Play Nine, Five Crowns (Aces wild), Sky-jo (colors, discard-stack rule), Golf Card Game (jokers, Canadian scoring); Skip-Bo and Skygo praised for it. Deviations are noticed by exactly the customers who own the box.
8. **Paid ad removal not honored, or moved to a subscription** — 6 apps: UNO, Skip-Bo, MobilityWare, Euchre 3D, Spades Plus, Play Nine.
9. **Forced accounts, updates, or mini-games** — 5 apps: Phase 10, Skip-Bo, MobilityWare, Exploding Kittens 2, Five Crowns.
10. **Timers and slow players with no kick** — 4 apps: Euchre 3D, Sky-jo, Exploding Kittens 2, Cabo.
11. **Card readability, small cards, touch misfires** — 4 apps: MobilityWare, Cribbage Pro, Slay the Spire, Skygo.
12. **Connection drops at the moment of winning** — 3 apps: Spades Plus, Cribbage Pro, Sky-jo.
13. **Missing house-rule variants** — Play Nine, Golf Card Game.

### (b) Praise themes recurring across tiers

1. **Plays with distant family** — 9 apps. The most emotionally loaded praise in the corpus: the 85-year-old grandmother's daily 5pm game, the dead father kept as a Euchre partner, Five Crowns fans awaiting multiplayer for family moving out of state.
2. **No ads, no IAP, one-time price** — Balatro, Slay the Spire, DEKÜ, Exploding Kittens 2, Grand Harvest (no video ads). Stated as relief: "it's nice to just play."
3. **Faithful to the physical game** — Skip-Bo, Play Nine, Five Crowns, Skygo, Golf Card Game, Exploding Kittens 2. "Exactly the same play as SkyJo" is a 3-star review only because the AI is weak.
4. **Quick rounds, low commitment** — Snap, DEKÜ, Play Nine, Golf Card Game, Skip-Bo.
5. **Offline play** — Slay the Spire, Balatro, DEKÜ, Skip-Bo (its removal drew 2-star).
6. **Daily ritual, keeps the brain sharp** — MobilityWare, Phase 10, Grand Harvest, DEKÜ, Trickster. Older players say this explicitly and are the ones who pay.
7. **Learning aids and stats** — Cribbage Pro (hints, discard grading), Euchre 3D; requested for Golf Card Game.
8. **House-rule customization** — Trickster, UNO; requested for Play Nine and Golf Card Game.
9. **Fair, tiered AI difficulty** — DEKÜ praised; requested for Sky-jo and Skygo.
10. **Responsive developer** — Five Crowns (in-app chat), Golf Card Game (email).

### (c) What reviewers say they would pay for, or paid for happily

- **A one-time price to remove ads permanently**, with numbers attached: "$5 - $10" (Play Nine), "$5 or even $10" (Sky-jo), "up to $7.99" (MobilityWare), "a small fee" (Five Crowns), "I would be willing to do that" (Trickster). The refusal is to subscriptions: Euchre 3D and Spades Plus users reject $10–20/month for what used to be a one-time purchase.
- **$9.99 for a complete, ad-free premium game**: Balatro and Slay the Spire buyers say it plainly, and several bought a second copy for mobile. The lone price objection ("$10 for a mobile game was a bit rich") is a 2022 outlier.
- **$4.99 for a brand adaptation as a companion to the physical box** (Exploding Kittens 2). The complaint is the $10–20 packs sold on top.
- **A season pass around $10** (Snap) is accepted; $50–100 bundles are not.
- Reviewers who paid for ad removal and still lost to "rigged" dealing (Phase 10), or saw ads return (UNO, Skip-Bo, Golf Card Game), call the purchase "a waste." Paying raises expectations of fairness, not just silence.

### (d) What triggers 1-star reviews from otherwise loyal users

1. **Ad removal revoked or converted to a subscription** — Euchre 3D's move from one-time Pro to $9.99/month is the clearest collapse in the set (54% recent 1-star on a 4.82 lifetime app).
2. **An update that shrinks cards, removes options, or breaks the daily habit** — MobilityWare ("I'm old. I want the same solitaire with my custom fixes"), Cribbage Pro's board redesign, Skip-Bo gating offline play.
3. **Discovering opponents are bots** — MobilityWare's fake chat, Skip-Bo's computer-speed "real players," Spades Plus bots with human IDs. Users feel deceived, not merely disappointed.
4. **Economy changes that devalue what was already bought or earned** — Snap's Spotlight system, Hearthstone rotation to Wild, Spades Plus removing the daily bonus wheel.
5. **A save or progression bug left unfixed for months** — Balatro's stake-win cloud-save bug is the sole reason a 4.96 app has 16% recent 1-stars; the "turn off cloud sync" workaround reads as contempt.
6. **Crashes immediately after an update** — Snap and Hearthstone in Sep 2026, Phase 10's latest build.
7. **Ads that punish** — sound over silent mode waking a baby, ads that get you kicked from a ranked game, ads that consume a wagered coin stake.
8. **Ads or paywalls added after an ad-free launch** — Five Crowns ("They hook you by no ads to begin. Then, more and more."), Exploding Kittens 2's paid packs on a paid app.
9. **Difficulty spikes that read as rigging** — Phase 10 past level 16, Cribbage Pro "Brutal," Play Nine last-player-wins perception.
