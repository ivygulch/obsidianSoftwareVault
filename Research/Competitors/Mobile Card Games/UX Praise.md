# Mobile Card Games — UX Praise


Scope: iOS App Store reviews (US storefront, "most helpful" sort, 50 per app; FR storefront for Belote.com) plus press, awards and designer-side sources. Only praise or criticism tied to a specific interaction or interface choice is recorded. Star rating and date are given for user quotes. Items marked "unverified" come from search snippets where the source page could not be fetched verbatim.

- Phase 10: praise limited to "it tells you exactly what you need every phase" (4★, 2023-07-10); complaints about forced tutorials and "100+ useless notification icons" (1★, 2025-06-25).
- Play Nine: "the app is beautifully designed" (1★, 2025-07-18) is the only interface remark; otherwise rigged-deal and coin complaints, plus a lobby visibility request (3★, 2025-11-25).
- Solitaire Grand Harvest: "so many other things going on like harvesting, scrapbooks, farm building ... one big chaotic scene" (4★, 2022-08-10).
- Spider Solitaire (MobilityWare): animation and background themes liked; complaints that columns no longer collapse on tap and that new animations ignore the reduce-animations setting (3★, 2018-12-16; 2★, 2025-06-20).
- Fairway Solitaire: "The app design itself is beautiful and clean." (5★, 2018-01-01); otherwise monetization complaints.

## 3. Observations

1. Across 30 apps, the most repeated user-voiced UI praise is for undo, hint, auto-complete and replay-the-same-deal in solitaire-family apps (MobilityWare, Microsoft, Fun Bridge, Cribbage Pro), not for novel gestures.
2. Portrait one-hand play is the most repeated UI request in landscape-only ports (Balatro, Pocket Card Jockey, Slay the Spire) and a stated purchase reason where present (Dawncaster, Card Crawl, Marvel Snap).
3. Animation is praised when short and skippable (Euchre 3D, Runeterra, Card Thief) and criticized when it blocks input or gates a Claim button (Marvel Snap, Exploding Kittens 2, Hearthstone menus, Card of Darkness).
4. Teaching features that users name are after-the-fact grading of a decision (Cribbage Pro), tap-to-explain before commit (Fun Bridge bids), and quests that teach by practice (Card Crawl); long forced tutorials are criticized (Pocket Card Jockey, Phase 10).
5. The only gesture innovations with critic and award recognition are Reigns' swipe-with-preview (Apple runner-up 2016), Pokémon TCG Pocket's guided pack rip (App Store Awards 2025) and Balatro's drag-to-zone (Apple Design Award 2025); Hearthstone's physical board is designer-voiced (GDC 2015).
6. Touch-target and occlusion complaints recur in card-hand layouts: fingers hide cards (Nertz JD, Slay the Spire), fanned hands are too small (Monster Train), taps land on adjacent cards (Microsoft Solitaire), and the iOS home indicator collides with the hand (Slay the Spire).
7. Themed card faces draw praise until they reduce rank or suit legibility (Solitaire Stories); Balatro's High Contrast Cards option is praised by critics as both accessibility and utility.
8. Video chat and private tables (Trickster) and resume-anywhere async play with VoiceOver support (King's Corner) are the named social and accessibility praise points among family-style card apps.
