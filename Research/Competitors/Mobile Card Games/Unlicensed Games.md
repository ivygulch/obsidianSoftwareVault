## 4. Observations

1. MobilityWare is the only publisher with the top app in five families: Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Pyramid and Crazy Eights. It also holds second place in Spades, Hearts and Gin Rummy, and its Rummy and Cribbage Daily apps launched in 2024 and 2025.
2. Trick-taking in the US splits between two publishers. Fiogonia (Spades *, Hearts, Euchre, Rummy 500, Canasta, Cribbage) has the largest rating counts at 4.55 to 4.64 stars; A Star Software (Spades+, Hearts+, Euchre 3D) is smaller at 4.79 to 4.82 stars. Zynga and Beach Bum sit third and fourth in Spades and Gin Rummy with social-casino monetization.
3. Real-money solitaire is now as large as classic solitaire by rating count. Solitaire Cash (577,902) and Solitaire Clash (459,526) both exceed FreeCell's top app. This category is not open to a premium indie app.
4. Games with no dedicated app above 3,000 US ratings: Oh Hell, President, War, Big Two, Old Maid, Go Fish, Thirty-One, Casino, Sheepshead (2,476), Five Hundred, Durak (top app rated 3.04). For Oh Hell, President, War and Speed, CardGames.io (52,291) is the top search result, so those games live inside multi-game apps.
5. Children's card games are not served. Go Fish, Old Maid and War have no dedicated app above 1,000 US ratings; the 455,255-rating "Go Fish!" is a Kwalee fishing arcade game, not the card game.
6. Premium pricing exists in Group A only at the tail and in Germany. US paid apps: Spite & Malice $2.99 (6,168), Extreme Hand and Foot $3.99 (4,106), Spades Pro $4.99 (1,547), Extreme 500 $2.99 (577), Pinochle HD $1.99 (558). Germany is the only storefront where a paid traditional card app has more than 10,000 ratings: Isar's Skat at €12.99 has 14,420, and Isar sells Doppelkopf and Schafkopf at €9.99 alongside free LITE versions.
7. Regional markets are each owned by one or two local publishers. GameDuell (Berlin) is first in FR belote (311,513, larger than any US trick-taking app except Spades *), FR tarot, FR rami and DE rommé. Isar Interactive is first in all three German trick-taking games, and Spiele-Palast holds third or fourth in every German game. Sorairo owns Japan's Daifugō (139,960). WhatWapp and OutOfTheBit split Italy. ZingPlay's portal is the only Vietnamese card app of size.
8. Balatro is the only original-IP premium card game above 10,000 US ratings (106,233 at $9.99 plus 63,547 on Arcade). The next premium originals are the Reigns titles at 7,000 to 15,000 each. Slay the Spire, Monster Train, Cultist Simulator and Card Crawl are all under 5,000 on iOS despite PC success.
9. Free-to-play original-IP CCGs skew to large publishers and mixed ratings: Hearthstone 121,514 at 4.15, MTG Arena 154,053 at 4.60. Legends of Runeterra and GWENT both stopped competitive development and remain listed. Cygames' 2025 Shadowverse relaunch has 1,186 ratings.
10. Licensed family games outscale every unlicensed multiplayer card game. UNO (652,151) and Phase 10 (606,350) each exceed Spades * (428,583), the largest unlicensed non-solitaire app. Skip-Bo (214,075) is comparable to Hearts and Gin Rummy leaders.
