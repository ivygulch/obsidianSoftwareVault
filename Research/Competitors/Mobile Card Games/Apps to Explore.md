# Mobile Card Games — Apps to Explore

## Priority 1 — study closely

1. **Balatro** ($9.99, or Balatro+ on Arcade). Apple Design Award 2025. Drag-to-zone controls, score digit roll, tiered feedback proportional to significance, layered sound, game-speed slider, High Contrast Cards, the Skip-button fat-finger placement, the landscape-only complaint.
2. **Play Nine: Golf Card Game** (free, ads). The closest golf-family comp. Invite-by-code friend rooms, 2–9 hole selection, opponent grid presentation, and every failure the reviews name: predictable bots, coin bleed, ads after each hole, no house rules.
3. **Sky-jo: Golf Card Game** (free, ads) and **Skygo: The Card Game** (free). The Skyjo clones. Wrong colors, discard-stack rule deviations, strategy-less AI, small cards, timers, coin wagers. The complaint list is the spec.
4. **Cribbage Pro** (free, $4.99 ad removal). Post-hand discard grading, hint system, statistics screen, published shuffle audits. The reference for learning aids and fairness transparency.
5. **Solitaire by MobilityWare** (free, or the $4.99 paid version). Tap-to-move and drag both supported, unlimited undo, hint, auto-complete with its too-early trigger, replay-same-deal, daily challenge calendar, skippable win animations, right/left-handed play.
6. **Marvel Snap** (free). Six-turn match tempo, nameplate-glow turn order, "Escaped!" instead of "you lose," 3D parallax card inspection, portrait one-thumb layout, and the blocking reward animations users hate.
7. **Hearthstone** (free). The physical board: consistent bounce and over-rotation across screens, card tilt on drag, board fidget objects, the burning-rope turn timer, sound authored to turn beats. Also the wasted-screen-space problem on iPhone.

## Priority 2 — one session each for a specific pattern

8. **Reigns** or **Reigns: Her Majesty** ($2.99). Hold-swipe preview before commit; accidental-commit cost.
9. **Card Crawl** (free, $3.99 unlock) and **Card Thief** ($2.99). Tinytouchtales' portrait one-thumb grid layouts, restrained animation, 2–5 minute rounds, HUD-under-notch mistake.
10. **Dawncaster** ($4.99). Portrait-native 60 fps card play; the "not a clunky PC port" praise.
11. **Trickster Cards** (free). House-rule configuration, private tables with join codes, video at the table, cross-platform accounts, bot-fill behavior.
12. **Euchre 3D** (free). Praised for restraint and uncluttered play; AI personalities; the ad-removal-to-subscription collapse.
13. **Microsoft Solitaire Collection** (free). Undo all, resume on relaunch, daily events, non-intrusive hint highlighting.
14. **Fun Bridge** (free). Tap a bid to see its meaning before committing; replay a hand with hints.
15. **King's Corner** (Bitpress, free). Resume-anywhere async, VoiceOver-playable grid.
16. **Pokémon TCG Pocket** (free). Guided-gesture pack rip (guide lines, delayed text hint, audio payoff), long-press immersive cards. Study the gesture teaching, not the economy.

## Priority 3 — negative controls (what to avoid, one look each)

17. **Slay the Spire** ($9.99). Cursor-emulation touch, cards cut off at the bottom, hand under the home indicator, inspect-vs-play conflict.
18. **Exploding Kittens 2** ($4.99). 3D card animations that slow play, drag-and-drop removed, mandatory account for multiplayer, no pass-and-play, $18 expansions on a paid app.
19. **Solitaire Grand Harvest** (free). The casino-economy template: fee-equals-payout levels, purchase pop-ups, "one big chaotic scene."
20. **Five Crowns: Kingdom Quest** (free). A live physical-brand adaptation launched single-player with ads added after launch; watch how the reviews turn.
21. **The Golf Card Game** (Glowing Eye, free, $3.99 upgrade). 4/6-card golf: house-rule requests (jokers, four-corners), dumb-or-lucky bots, ads shown to paying users.

## Regional (optional, for DE/FR launch)

22. **Belote.com** (GameDuell, FR storefront). Persistent trump visibility; the French folk-game leader.
23. **Skat LITE / Skat** (Isar Interactive, DE storefront, €12.99 paid). The only paid traditional card app above 10K ratings anywhere; a German premium precedent.
