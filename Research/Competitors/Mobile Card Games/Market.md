## Trends

- Solitaire is the volume and revenue engine of mobile card games. 348M downloads and $470.2M gross spend in 2024; spend nearly doubled since 2019 ($237.7M) though below the 2023 peak of $564.7M. Jan to May 2025: 163.8M downloads, $204.3M spend; April 2025 set the all-time monthly download record (33.5M) and May 2025 the spend record ($53M). Playtika, King, Scopely, Tripledot, Zynga, Metacore and Superplay all now ship solitaire titles; the stated reasons are organic search traffic, low dev cost and IP leverage. Source: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/why-are-the-worlds-top-games-publishers-making-solitaire-games-and-has-playtika-already-won/ (Sensor Tower data, Jun 2025).
- Solitaire Grand Harvest is the template the majors are copying: 2-minute TriPeaks sessions, level progression, a casino-style scaling economy and variable bets. $1B lifetime by Feb 2025 on 69M downloads. Sources: https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-solitaire-grand-harvest-hits-1bn-earnings-from-rovio-roblox-krafton-and-square-more/ and https://naavik.co/deep-dives/solitaire-grand-harvest-deconstruction/.
- The Balatro effect is real but narrow. Balatro is the reference case for a $9.99 no-IAP mobile card game: $4.4M in two months, top of the paid charts, and it still sits at #2 paid Card two years later. Slay the Spire II entered early access March 2026; Apple Arcade now carries Balatro+, Slay the Spire+ and UNO Arcade Edition as its card anchors. Sources: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/balatro-nears-44m-on-mobile-amid-a-sudden-spending-surge/ and https://www.pocketgamer.biz/playstack-profit-jumps-59-as-balatro-and-abiotic-factor-drive-record-sales/.
- Collectible IP is the only card segment growing at scale, and it is one title. Pokémon TCG Pocket ($0.99B IAP in 2025, third-highest IP-based mobile title) carries the CCG category while Marvel Snap and Hearthstone decline. Sources: https://sensortower.com/blog/pokemon-tcg-pocket-100-million-downloads, https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/marvel-snap, https://gameluster.com/marvel-snap-second-dinner-layoffs-2026/, https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/hearthstone-player-count.
- Licensed family card brands are consolidating under brand owners. Mattel took Mattel163 fully in-house (550M downloads, 20M MAU, $318M valuation) and plans two games a year; Bonfit self-publishes Play Nine and now Five Crowns. Sources: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mattel-acquires-full-ownership-of-mattel163-from-netease-for-159m/ and https://mobilegamer.biz/data-digest-netease-sells-stake-in-mattel163-unity-results-socialpoints-1bn-installs-liftoff-ipo-stalls-more/.
- Real-money "skill" solitaire is under legal pressure. Papaya (Solitaire Cash) lost a $719M judgment to Skillz over undisclosed bots and filed Chapter 15 in Aug 2026, while its app still ranks #2 Casino. Source: https://www.casino.org/news/papaya-files-chapter-15-bankruptcy-concedes-it-cant-pay-719m-judgment/.
- Hybrid-casual growth is in puzzle, not card. AppMagic's 2025 hybrid-casual top-10 contains no card titles. Source: https://appmagic.rocks/blog/q3hybrid2025. Macro: global mobile game IAP grew only 0.2% in 2025 (https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-gaming-2026).
- Premium board and card adaptations are a profitable niche for one mid-size studio. Marmalade Game Studio doubled EBITDA to £5.2M on £12.7M FY25 revenue selling $4.99 to $9.99 licensed adaptations with DLC. Source: https://www.ldc.co.uk/news/marmalade-game-studio-fy25-results/.

## Takeaways for a premium family card game

- Every mass-market licensed family card app (UNO!, Phase 10, Skip-Bo, Rummikub, Monopoly Solitaire, Play Nine, Five Crowns) is F2P with ads and coin IAP; there is no premium, ad-free entry in that tier on the US store today.
- The premium card titles that rank (Balatro #2 paid Card, Exploding Kittens 2 #10 paid Card, Dawncaster #8 paid Card) sit at $4.99 to $9.99 and pair the upfront price with either zero IAP or cosmetic and expansion DLC only.
- Balatro's 106K US ratings on a $9.99 app show a paying audience for card games far larger than the indie deckbuilder norm (Dawncaster 923, Card Crawl Adventure 298), but the gap between it and the next premium title is two orders of magnitude.
- Play Nine, the closest golf-family comparable with a brand behind it, has 38,879 US ratings after five years as F2P with a $9.99/yr VIP sub; it is the largest golf-family app on either store.
- The Skyjo name on iOS is owned by clones and score keepers: the top clone has 3,269 US ratings, the second-best 98, and there is no official Magilano app.
- Solo-dev folk card apps (Euchre 3D, Spades+, Cribbage Pro, Hearts Deluxe) sustain 60K to 180K US ratings and 4.7 to 4.9 stars over a decade or more on ads plus a single lifetime ad-removal SKU of $4.99 to $19.99.
- The solitaire genre's growth is driven by live-ops F2P with casino-style economies and 18+ ratings; the "relaxing family card game" positioning in those apps' copy does not match their monetization.
- Apple Arcade has become the home for ad-free versions of the same card IP (Balatro+, Slay the Spire+, UNO Arcade Edition), which is a distribution route that carries no ads or IAP by design.
- Age rating splits the market: UNO!, Phase 10, Skip-Bo, Play Nine and Skygo are 4+; the solitaire majors, Trickster, VIP Spades and Cribbage Pro are 13+ or 18+ because of simulated gambling or chat.
- Five Crowns: Kingdom Quest (May 2026) launched single-player only with multiplayer "coming," and reached #108 Family within four months on a brand smaller than Skyjo's.
