Your posting describes a native engineer who ships consumer-facing features end to end in the Thumbtack customer and pro apps, owns them through experimentation and post-launch support, and lands partner integrations inside those apps. I have shipped on both UIKit and SwiftUI and chosen between them feature by feature; that judgment is part of what I would bring.

On product sense and partners: the fire-department app began as a non-technical customer's description of how volunteer departments coordinate an incident, and I translated that into formal specifications before writing code, iterating with the customer and department stakeholders. My AI work has been on the development side: evaluating tools in Walmart's engineering working groups, assisting the platform team in integrating Builder.io and training more than 80 iOS engineers on it, and using Claude Code and Codex daily since.
