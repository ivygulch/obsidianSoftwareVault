**Subject: Inquiry — digital game rights for SKYJO**

Dear Herr Bernhardt,

My name is [colleague name]. I am writing on behalf of my longtime colleague Doug Sjoquist and his US company, Ivy Gulch LLC, in which I am the German-speaking representative. First, congratulations on SKYJO — it is a rare thing to design a game that families across the world genuinely love.

Our question, stated plainly: are the digital game rights to SKYJO currently licensed? And if not, would you be open to a conversation about an official mobile adaptation?

Why we ask: SKYJO's fans have no official digital home, and they are visibly asking for one. The leading unofficial imitation on Apple's App Store has gathered more than 3,000 ratings in the United States and nearly as many in France — yet its recent reviews have collapsed, with players describing intrusive advertising that interrupts games, constant errors, and computer opponents that play senselessly. Many write outright that they would gladly pay for a proper version. That is your game's audience, being poorly served under your game's name.

What we would propose, in brief: an official adaptation for iPhone and Android, faithful to your design in every detail — the rules, the card colors, the feel — with skillful computer opponents, German, French, and English from day one, and no advertising of any kind. Premium quality, sold at a fair one-time price, under a royalty arrangement with Magilano. As the app matures, we could add  family play across distance and other features for your audience. We have already built a working prototype of the gameplay and would be glad to show it to you.

Doug has built iOS software professionally for fifteen years — most recently as Principal Software Engineer at Walmart, responsible for the checkout experience in an app serving five to six million daily users — and has shipped more than twenty apps. [One sentence on colleague's background.]

Would you have thirty minutes for a call? And if the rights are already committed elsewhere, we would be grateful to know — it is a genuine compliment to your game that we are asking.

With best regards,

[Colleague name]
on behalf of Douglas Sjoquist, Ivy Gulch LLC
[colleague email] · dwsjoquist@ivygulch.com
