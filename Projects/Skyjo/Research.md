## Licensing frame (from the landscape note)

- Mechanics = folk game "Golf," unownable; the license buys the trademark, search capture, Apple trademark-dispute enforcement, featuring eligibility, the rulebook-QR funnel, and price power.
- Enforcement stays contractually the trademark owner's job; licensee duty = notify (in practice: filing Apple metadata complaints, hours per quarter).
- Comparables: Root ($9.99 / 5,457 ratings / 4.87★), Handelabra's Spirit Island (small-studio sustainability), Temple Gates (6–8 year long-tail sales after abandonment).
