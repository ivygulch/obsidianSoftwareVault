# App Comps

Card games with a Skyjo-like vibe (casual, family, kitchen-table physical brands) and official App Store presence. All figures US storefront via the appstore MCP, 2026-08-14 — ratings counts and scores are point-in-time snapshots. Monetization framing per [[Projects/Skyjo/Design|Design]]: no ads ever, purchased game always fully playable without IAP; beyond that the comps are a spectrum, not models to copy or reject wholesale.

## Closest comp — official golf-mechanic family card game

| App | Publisher | Model | Rating | US ratings | Released |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Play Nine: Golf Card Game](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/play-nine-golf-card-game/id1567060312) | Bonfit America (the physical game's own publisher) | Free + IAP | 4.81 | 38,243 | 2021 |

Skyjo is a golf-family game; Play Nine is the same story one step ahead — best-selling physical golf card game, publisher-owned official app, solo-vs-AI + play-with-friends-by-code + online multiplayer, family-safe positioning, physical-game cross-sell in the description. Actively updated. The single most instructive comp for feature set and tone. Same studio shipped **Five Crowns: Kingdom Quest** (2025) — the physical-family-card-game-to-app playbook being repeated.

## Premium / paid — closest to the working model

| App | Publisher | Price | Rating | US ratings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Exploding Kittens® 2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/exploding-kittens-2/id6478287619) | Marmalade (official license) | $4.99 | 4.75 | 3,709 (year one) |
| [That's Pretty Clever](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/thats-pretty-clever/id1411348057) | BrettspielWelt (Schmidt Spiele license) | $3.99 | 3.93 | 220 |
| Clever sequels (Twice as Clever, Clever Mixed) | BrettspielWelt | $3.99 | 3.4–4.0 | small |

Exploding Kittens 2 validates $4.99 premium for a casual licensed card game (Marmalade's whole catalog — Monopoly, Clue, Game of Life — is this model). The Clever series is the **German-publisher precedent** (Schmidt Spiele licensing a beloved hit to a small digital studio) — directly relevant to the Magilano conversation — and simultaneously a cautionary tale: sub-4.0 ratings on a beloved game show a mediocre official app damages the brand it licenses. That cuts both ways in the outreach argument. Root ($9.99, 5,457 ratings) and Handelabra's Spirit Island round out the hobbyist end — see [[Licensed IP Game Adaptations — Landscape]].

## Instructive absences

Qwixx (NSV), Cabo, Skull King, and Wizard have **no official iOS app** — only score-sheet companions and low-quality clones. Kosmos's Lost Cities, long the premium-adaptation poster child, no longer appears in US search results (only third-party companion apps). The Skyjo situation — huge physical brand, zero official digital presence, clone-occupied search — is common, which strengthens the "real, repeatable licensing gap" framing, and Bonfit's Play Nine → Five Crowns repetition shows someone else is already running this playbook.
