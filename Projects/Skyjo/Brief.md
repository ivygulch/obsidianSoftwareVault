# Skyjo — Brief

## Concept

An official, licensed mobile adaptation of SKYJO for iOS and Android: premium quality, no ads, faithful to the physical game, with skillful computer opponents and family play across distance. The venture licenses the SKYJO brand from Magilano (Alexander Bernhardt, the game's designer and owner) under a royalty arrangement; the mechanics themselves are folk-game "Golf" and unownable — the license buys the name, the search traffic, the trademark enforcement lever, and the physical-to-digital funnel.

## Why now

- Proven demand with no official supply: the leading unofficial clone holds 3,100+ US App Store ratings and as many again in France (2,043 Germany), and "skyjo" is a heavily searched navigational term.
- The incumbent is collapsing: its newest reviews average ~2.3★, dominated by ad-hijacking, freezes, and senseless bots — and players state a $5–10 willingness to pay for a proper version.
- The build economics work for a small team: a rules-faithful prototype with trained AI opponents took two days (August 2026) with AI-assisted development.

## Success criteria

- License secured on terms with a workable royalty, term length, and sunset provisions.
- Shipped official app (iOS first, Android to follow fast) at premium price, DE/FR/EN at launch.
- Portfolio-member economics: a modest durable revenue stream (Handelabra/Spirit Island comp), not a salary-replacement bet — per the portfolio income model.

## Risks

- Rights unavailable or already committed (binary gate; one email to find out).
- Renewal/licensor dependency — negotiate term + sunset up front.
- Clones persist regardless (license enables brand enforcement, not eradication).
- Multiplayer implies ongoing ops cost — async-first design bounds it.
- Counterparty is a creator, not a corporation: fidelity and tone matter more than terms in the first conversation.

## Out of scope (v1)

Real-time multiplayer; web client; markets beyond DE/FR/EN localization; F2P/ads monetization (permanently out).
