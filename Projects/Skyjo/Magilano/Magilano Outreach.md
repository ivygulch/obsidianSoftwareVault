## Email draft (v2)

**Subject: Inquiry — digital game rights for SKYJO**

Dear Magilano team,

My name is Douglas Sjoquist. I'm a US-based software developer, and I'm writing to ask about the status of digital game rights for SKYJO: whether they are currently licensed, and if not, whether you would be open to a conversation about an official mobile adaptation.

Briefly, my background: I've built iOS apps professionally for fifteen years, most recently as a Principal Software Engineer at Walmart, where I was responsible for the checkout experience in an app serving five to six million daily users. Independently, I've shipped more than twenty apps to the App Store. I work with a small team and build at production quality.

Why I'm asking: SKYJO has one of the most devoted casual audiences of any modern card game, and right now that audience has no official digital home. The demand is easy to see — the leading unofficial imitation has accumulated more than 3,000 ratings in the US App Store, and nearly as many again in France.

What those players find there, though, is hurting the game's good name. That imitation's recent reviews have collapsed — of its 200 most recent US reviews, well over half are one or two stars — with players describing advertisements that hijack the screen mid-game, constant freezes, and computer opponents that play senselessly. Many say outright that they would gladly pay $5–10 for a proper version without ads. Your fans are asking publicly, in someone else's reviews, for exactly the official SKYJO app that doesn't exist.

What I would propose, at a high level: a polished, family-friendly official adaptation for iOS and Android — solo play against skillful computer opponents, pass-and-play, and online play designed for families playing across distance. Premium quality with no advertising, built to the details your players ask for: the official card colors, faithful rules, unhurried play, and accessibility for every age. Official expansions such as SKYJO Action could follow as in-app additions, and I'd want to explore connecting the physical game to the app — for example, a QR code in the rulebook — so every box you sell introduces players to the official app.

If the rights are available, I'd welcome a short call to discuss what a license could look like. And if they're already committed elsewhere, I'd be grateful to know that too — it's a genuine compliment to the game that I'm asking.

Thank you for your time, and congratulations on what you've built with SKYJO.

Best regards,
Douglas Sjoquist
Ivy Gulch LLC
dwsjoquist@ivygulch.com
