## English translation

Dear Herr Bernhardt, dear Sir or Madam,

I would like to introduce myself with this email. I am writing on behalf of my longtime colleague Doug Sjoquist and his company Ivy Gulch LLC, at which I am the German-speaking representative. First of all, congratulations on SKYJO — it is quite rare to design a game that families across the whole world so genuinely love.

Our question, stated plainly: are the digital rights to SKYJO already licensed? If not, would you be open to a conversation about an official mobile adaptation?

We ask because SKYJO fans currently have no official digital home, and they are openly asking for one. The leading unofficial imitation has already gathered 3,000 ratings in the United States, and nearly as many in France. Yet its recent reviews have collapsed. Players report disruptive and intrusive advertising that interrupts play, unceasing errors, and computer opponents that play senselessly. Many write openly that they would gladly pay for a genuine version of the game. We are talking here about the fans of your game — customers who are currently not being properly served in your game's name.

We would like to propose the following: we develop an official adaptation of your game for iPhone and Android — faithful in every respect to the rules, the card colors, the artwork, and the feel of the game — with skillful computer opponents in German, French, and English from day one — without any advertising whatsoever. Outstanding quality, sold at a fair price, with a royalty arrangement with Magilano. All development would be at our expense and our risk — Magilano licenses the rights and receives royalties — that's it. We have already developed a prototype and would be glad to show it to you.

Doug (Herr Sjoquist) has been building iOS software for 15 years, most recently as Principal Software Engineer at Walmart, responsible for the checkout experience within an app serving five to six million daily users. Beyond that, he has created more than 20 apps. For my part, I have spent decades building software and providing quality assurance for iOS, Android, and web-based software for major Swiss transportation companies and government agencies — delivered, as is customary in Switzerland, in German, French, Italian, and English.

Would you have 30 minutes for a call? And should the rights already be committed elsewhere, we would be grateful if you could let us know. Please take our inquiry as a compliment. Your game and its fans would be in good hands with us.

With kind regards,

Cliff Gertiser
on behalf of Doug Sjoquist, Ivy Gulch LLC
cliff.gertiser@ivygulch.com · dwsjoquist@ivygulch.com
