# Alexander Bernhardt

Profile of SKYJO's designer and Magilano's principal — the counterparty for the SKYJO digital-rights outreach. All information from public sources, compiled 2026-08-11.

## Who he is

- **Designer and artist of SKYJO** (first published 2015). Credited on [BoardGameGeek](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/93240/alexander-bernhardt) as both designer and artist; US retail brands the game "[Skyjo by Alexander Bernhardt](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/skyjo-by-alexander-bernhardt-magilano/1146366154)" — his name is on the box.
- **Founder-principal of Magilano**, the game's publisher. Named as the sole responsible person on the company [Impressum](https://magilano.com/en/pages/impressum). A founder-designer running his own family-scale publisher — likely a single decision-maker.
- **Also designed SKYJO Action** (2019), the official expansion/variant.

## Public profile

- **Low-key**: no interviews found in English or German searches (2026-08-11). He lets the game speak.

This strengthens the outreach argument: the digital space around his game is 100% occupied by parties he doesn't control.

## Name etymology (curiosity, not fact)

The origin of "Magilano" is not publicly documented. Speculation only: it resembles (but does not match) the Italian surname/toponym "Magliano"; a tempting-but-unconfirmed decomposition is Magi- (Magie/Magier, German for magic/magician) + an Italianate ending. Could equally be personal. Good warm-up question for an eventual conversation — founders enjoy telling the naming story.

## Outreach implications

- The outreach goes to **the creator personally**, not a licensing department: the brand-protection argument (clones abusing SKYJO's name and quality) is about *his* name and *his* game. Personal, not corporate.
- A creator will weigh fidelity, quality, and tone more heavily than royalty mechanics — the pitch should lead with faithfulness to his design and protection of his brand, with money second.
- Address him directly (Herr Bernhardt), in German — per the plan, the colleague sends the outreach as Doug's representative ([[Magilano Outreach]]).
- Single decision-maker means the first impression effectively *is* the negotiation posture.
