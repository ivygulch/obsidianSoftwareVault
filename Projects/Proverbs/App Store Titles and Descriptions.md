# Proverbs — App Store Titles and Descriptions

> Title and description research for naming the [[Projects/Proverbs/Ideal State|Proverbs]] app and writing its listing. US App Store, fetched 2026-10-26 through the appstore MCP tools (search, app details, keyword scores). Builds on the 2026-10-03 competitor scan in [[Proverbs & Situational Scripture Apps — Landscape]]; apps new since then are marked. Keyword difficulty and traffic figures are the tool's own heuristic scores on a 0 to 10 scale, not measured search volume. The tool does not return subtitles or store categories, so those two fields remain unmeasured. Rating counts are the tool's `reviews` field.

## Searches run

`proverbs` (44 results), `book of proverbs` (30), `daily proverb` (23), `wisdom bible` (29), `proverbs devotional` (28), `solomon wisdom` (20), `proverbs study` (18), `bible verses by topic` (19), plus Google Play `proverbs bible` (9). Roughly half of the `proverbs` results are not the biblical book: African, Akan, Twi, Afghan, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, and Arabic folk-proverb apps, English idiom trainers, quote apps, and the Proverbs 31 women's devotionals.

Formulas in use:

- `Proverbs + cadence word`: Proverb-A-Day, Proverbs Daily, Todays Proverbs, Daily Bible Proverbs, Proverb of the Day (folk, not biblical).
- `Virtue word: Proverbs qualifier`: Daily Wisdom: Book of Proverbs, Wisdom: Daily Proverbs Verse.
- `Proverbs: translation + virtue`: Proverbs Daily: KJV Wisdom.
- `Verb + Proverbs`: Memorize Proverbs.
- `Proverbs + sibling book`: Proverbs & Psalms, Daily: Psalms & Proverbs.
- `Possessive`: Solomon's Wisdom.
- `Proverbs - Devotional title`: Proverbs - Under Construction.

No title in the set uses any of: topic, situation, tag, search, find, map, cloud, link, study, guide, index. The only "by topic" titles found anywhere are Oaysis: Bible Verses By Topic (whole Bible, 12 topics, last updated 2022, zero ratings) and African Proverbs by Topic (folk proverbs).

Name collisions: no app titled exactly "Proverbs" was returned by any search, but "Proverb" (singular) exists at $0.99 and "Proverbs++" holds the `/app/proverbs/` URL slug. Apple enforces unique app names at submission; exact availability of "Proverbs" can only be confirmed in App Store Connect. "Solomon's Wisdom", "Proverbs Daily", "Daily Wisdom", and "Wisdom" are taken as names.

## 3. Description anatomy

**Openers.** Two patterns. A one-sentence hook about the practice: "One proverb a day, one honest question, one line in your journal." (Proverbs Daily); "A chapter of Proverbs a day, read or read aloud." (Solomon's Wisdom); "One book, start to finish." (Memorize Proverbs); "Quiet the noise. Focus the mind." (Wisdom). Or a paragraph summarizing the book itself: Proverbs & Psalms spends its whole description on the structure and authorship of the two books and never describes the app; Todays Proverbs and Proverb open the same way.

**The chapter-a-day calendar** is claimed by six apps (Proverbs++, Solomon's Wisdom, Memorize Proverbs, Under Construction, Daily: Psalms & Proverbs, Todays Proverbs) in nearly identical sentences: "Proverbs has 31 chapters, so the book already matches the calendar." It is the default habit hook in this niche.

**"915 verses"** appears verbatim in three descriptions (Proverbs Daily, Memorize Proverbs, Under Construction) as a completeness claim.

**Negative lists.** The 2025 to 2026 solo cohort converges on a "what it isn't" block: no ads, no account, no analytics, no tracking, works offline, no streaks or "no price, no subscription". Memorize Proverbs has the longest ("There is no verse library here, no way to add other passages, and no plan to expand to other books. It does one thing."); Proverbs Daily ("No streaks. Missing a day costs nothing. The verse is patient."); Wisdom ("no settings to configure, no social feeds to check, and no streaks to maintain"); Under Construction ("Private. Offline. Quiet.").

**Translation named.** 13 of 17 descriptions name the text. Two attribution styles: a formal copyright line (Memorize Proverbs reproduces Lockman's NASB notice; this is a solo developer shipping a complete licensed book with "Used by permission"), and a plain list of versions. Proverbs Daily names a public-domain text per language (Statenvertaling, Louis Segond 1910, Lutherbibel 1912, Diodati, Reina-Valera 1909).

**Pricing paragraphs.** The pattern for free-plus-paid apps is a paragraph headed "Free and Pro" that lists what stays free, then the paid tiers, then "Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase." Solomon's Wisdom: "The app is free and always will be. Tips are optional and unlock nothing." Daily: Psalms & Proverbs: "for a one-time purchase, you unlock sick lock screen + home screen widgets."

**Feature vocabulary.** Widgets (Lock Screen, Home Screen) in 7; narration or text-to-speech in 4; journal or notes in 6; favorites or bookmarks in 6; reminders in 6; share as card in 3; iCloud sync in 2; AI chat in 1 (EverydAI). Nobody describes tags, links between passages, a history or trail, or user-built structure.

**Length.** From one sentence (DAILY BIBLE PROVERBS) to about 450 words (Memorize Proverbs, Proverbs Daily). The two most recent and most carefully written listings (Proverbs Daily, Memorize Proverbs) are the long ones, in short paragraphs with em-dash or bullet feature lists and a closing attribution or privacy line.

## 5. What no listing describes

Against the ISA's intent, these are absent from every title and description found: categorization of passages by situation, attitude, trait, or result; tags on passages; links between passages with any weighting; a navigable history or tree of a reading session; user-defined groups; a cloud or map as the entry point; version-specific tagging. The closest phrases in the field are Oaysis's "Finding the best bible verse for a situation can be difficult to locate quickly. Each category ... is beautifully organized by topic" and EverydAI's "Ask the AI Chat—meanings, summaries, cross-themes, or practical steps."
