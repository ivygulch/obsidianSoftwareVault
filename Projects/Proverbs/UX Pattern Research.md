# Applying Proverbs — UX Pattern Research

> UX examples from life-advice, wisdom, devotional, quote, reflection, and card-draw apps, organized by interaction pattern for [[Applying Proverbs]]. Store listings and reviews fetched 2026-10-03; screenshot URLs come from the iTunes Lookup API. Feature descriptions beyond listings and reviews are marked (unverified) where the web search budget ran out.

Scope: iOS life-advice, devotional, journaling, quote, flash-card, tarot, and note apps; US App Store listings and reviews gathered 2026-10-03. Catalog, not a recommendation. Quotes are verbatim with stars and date; rating counts are US totals at fetch time. *(unverified)* marks feature claims from product knowledge or listing copy not confirmed on-device (WebSearch budget was exhausted).

## 1. Capturing "what is going on"

**Daylio** (4.77, 61,850) — Two-tap logging: a five-face mood row, then a grid of activity icons; free text is optional and comes last. Custom moods and activities use emoji; grouping of activities is a paid feature.
- 5★ 2019-03-14: "I wish there was a way to track mood changes throughout a day, or to write new notes instead of editing or starting over for a day."
- 5★ 2021-12-20: "having to endlessly scroll through the long list of 2500+ emojis to find the one I want to represent the activity. I'd appreciate if they added an option to search through the emojis."

**Motivation – Daily Quotes** (4.82, 1.07M) — Full-screen quote cards, vertical swipe (TikTok convention) to the next quote, heart to save, share sheet, topic filters and custom fonts/backgrounds.
- 5★ 2025-09-30: "Sometimes I can scroll through and every quote for a solid ten minutes is saved to my images, and I have to make myself stop and step away from the iPhone lol."
- 2★ 2026-03-28: "I highly disagree with not being able to have access to the quotes that I've liked…"

- 5★ 2020-12-07: "I love that after you draw your daily cards you can click on them and they'll have the meaning and few keywords associated with the card."
- 3★ 2024-11-16: "I seemed to have been forced to use only the Sacral Iris Tarot for daily draws when I usually used the Golden Thread Tarot."

**Oblique Strategies SE** (4.82, 553, $3.99) — One card, one button (new card), optional shake-to-draw *(unverified)*, plus a widget that shows a card.

**Readwise** (4.81, 1,513) — Daily Review presents a fixed number of highlights one per screen; horizontal swipe advances; per-card actions include favorite, discard ("never show again"), tag, note, and a frequency control ("show more/less often") *(button names unverified)*.
- 5★ 2020-05-01: "It also lets you design your highlights for 'active recall' and choose how often something resurfaces."
- 5★ 2025-06-20: "the Daily Review has been super-charged with an audio version…"

- 5★ 2018-04-19: "I also appreciate that the place where I got the word wrong stays shaded in for a few reviews to help me remember. I appreciate that the app gives grace for typos."
- 5★ 2023-11-29: "It learns how well you actually have memorized it, and calculates how often you should review it."
- Screens: https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/image/thumb/PurpleSource221/v4/91/05/8f/91058f30-7556-a694-386d-87dc7bcedca9/6.9-Screenshot-2.png/320x480bb.jpg · https://is1-ssl.mzstatic.com/image/thumb/PurpleSource221/v4/7c/70/04/7c700458-66a7-625f-af66-65f8853b1ee3/6.9-Screenshot-10.png/320x480bb.jpg

**AnkiMobile** (4.04, 2,346, $24.99) — Tap to reveal, then four graded buttons (Again/Hard/Good/Easy); the low rating is about price and desktop-first UI, not the gesture.

- 5★ 2020-11-10: "Over time it has expanded into several different journals."

## Patterns with no good example found

- Voice-first situation capture with structured output (feeling + people + topic) — Apple Journal and Day One record audio; neither parses it into tags.
- Wiki links between items of a fixed corpus (passage ↔ passage) on a phone — Obsidian/Craft/Bear link user-authored notes; no devotional or quote app sampled lets a user link two passages.
- "Pull a card for this question" against a tagged corpus — Labyrinthos does it for tarot; no scripture or quote app sampled offers draw-for-a-situation.
- Widget-side actions (save/dismiss from the widget) and widget/in-app card sync — requested by Streaks and Oblique Strategies reviewers; no sampled app demonstrates either.
- AI answer with per-claim source chips that open the primary text in-app — Perplexity does web sources; Bible Chat and Haven render verse references but no review or screenshot confirms tap-through to the passage in context.
